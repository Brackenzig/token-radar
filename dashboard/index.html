How a launch is priced: log10(market cap) = 3.399 + 0.478 × log10(TVL), fitted at build time from the 101 traded tokens with TVL of $10M or more (R² 0.16); each protocol's implied value is its curve prediction × its category factor × its week's growth × momentum × maturity × breadth. Leave-one-out: median miss ×3.86, within 3× 42%, within 10× 77%, against ×5.03 / 39% / 74% for one flat mcap/TVL multiple. Time split (fit before 2024, test the 40 listed since): ×3.16 / 48% / 80% against ×5.10 / 40% / 75% flat. Every band on the board is the P10–P90 of 200 bootstrap refits. No name is ever hardcoded — every factor is fitted from this snapshot, and a protocol whose token already trades is kept off the board.

The slice is a tenth of the protocol's implied launch value, pro-rated to the deposit against its TVL. The net is the slice minus fees, gas and the yield given up by parking the capital. FARM means $5 or more net per hands-on hour, THIN means $0 to $5, SKIP means below $0. The capital control re-nets the slice, the net and the verdict at the chosen deposit; the steps are written for the $1,000 plan, and only the deposit amount scales.
